Forge the Referer — server-side

The browser wouldn't let a webpage fake this header. So this page hands the job to a small server, which makes the request with whatever Referer you type and shows you the reply.

Why this works and the browser lab didn't. The request now leaves from a server, not your browser tab. Servers aren't bound by the browser's forbidden-header rule or by CORS, so the forged Referer arrives intact — and the response comes back readable.
